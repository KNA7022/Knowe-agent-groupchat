  - **The plan asked for USearch; the implementation does not use it, and the reason is a measurement.** `usearch` 2.26.2 is a 0.33 MB wheel under Apache-2.0 — but `usearch.index.Index.add` asserts `isinstance(vectors, np.ndarray)` and importing the package pulls numpy in. So numpy is a hard prerequisite for either approach, and measured on its own it costs **+14.92 MB** frozen. What usearch adds on top of that is *approximation*, and approximation spends recall, while the acceptance target is Recall@10 ≥ 0.85 — exact scan gets 100% for free.

At personal-knowledge-base size (thousands to tens of thousands of chunks) an exact scan answers in tens of milliseconds against LLM calls measured in seconds. Buying back that time buys nothing.
