  - **The plan asked for USearch; the implementation does not use it, and the reason is a measurement.** `usearch` 2.26.2 is a 0.33 MB wheel under Apache-2.0 — but `usearch.index.Index.add` asserts `isinstance(vectors, np.ndarray)` and importing the package pulls numpy in.
